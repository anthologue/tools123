2-link robot arm

A robot arm made of two straight pieces (links) joined at an elbow. Choose how long each link is and how far each joint turns, and see where the tip of the arm ends up. Working out the tip position from the joint angles is called forward kinematics.

Settings

cm

The first piece, from the base (the shoulder) to the elbow. 1 to 50 cm.

cm

The second piece, from the elbow to the tip. 1 to 50 cm.

°

Measured from the x-axis: 0° = link 1 points right, 90° = straight up, −90° = straight down.

°

Measured from link 1: 0° = the arm is straight, positive = bends anticlockwise, negative = bends clockwise.

Step-by-step working-out

To follow along on a calculator, set it to degrees (DEG) mode.
